<h2 class="text-xl font-semibold text-foreground mt-10 mb-3">Common mistakes: Why some fleet operators still get burned by their shop</h2>
<p class="text-base leading-relaxed">Most small fleet operators know they should be doing this differently. The gap is usually one of these five mistakes, and each one is fixable once you know what to look for.</p>
<ul class="list-disc pl-6 space-y-2 text-base leading-relaxed">
  <li><strong>Treating every repair as a one-off transaction.</strong> Walking in cold every time means no shop knows your fleet's history. A recurring coolant leak that gets "fixed" four times by four different techs at four different shops never actually gets fixed. A consistent shop sees the pattern.</li>
  <li><strong>Skipping DOT annual inspections until the last minute.</strong> If your annual inspection lapses, your truck is technically non-compliant from day one past the sticker date. A planned <a class="text-primary hover:underline font-medium" style="color: #DC2626;" href="/dot-inspections-phoenix-az">DOT inspection</a> takes a few hours; a compliance violation costs multiples of that in fines and lost operating authority.</li>
  <li><strong>Not asking for written PM reminders.</strong> When you're dispatching 10 trucks, tracking individual PM intervals manually falls apart. Demand that your shop send reminders before intervals lapse — not after.</li>
  <li><strong>Ignoring trailer maintenance until it's structural.</strong> Trailer lighting faults, brake issues, and air-ride problems are some of the most common OOS triggers at inspections. A shop that handles your tractors but ignores your trailers is only doing half the job. For fleet operators in Glendale, Tolleson, or Goodyear running tight dispatch windows, <a class="text-primary hover:underline font-medium" style="color: #DC2626;" href="/mobile-repair">mobile repair</a> can keep a truck earning while it's serviced.</li>

<h2 class="text-xl font-semibold text-foreground mt-10 mb-3">Pro strategies: Get more from your fleet maintenance program</h2>
<h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-foreground mt-6 mb-2">Stack your PM visits with DOT annual inspections</h3>
<p class="text-base leading-relaxed">Every truck needs a <a class="text-primary hover:underline font-medium" style="color: #DC2626;" href="/dot-inspections-phoenix-az">DOT annual inspection</a> under FMCSA 49 CFR Part 396. If you're already pulling a truck for a major PM — think 100,000-mile service — schedule the annual inspection the same visit. You're already paying for the tech's time on the truck; stack the inspection on top and leave with both the PM record and the sticker in one shot. CT Shop is a certified Federal Annual Inspection station and issues stickers on the spot when a truck passes.</p>
<h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-foreground mt-6 mb-2">Use diagnostics proactively, not just when a fault code lights up</h3>
<p class="text-base leading-relaxed">Modern Cummins, Detroit, and Volvo engines log fault codes that never trigger the dash warning — they sit in pending status and give you a window to act before they become active failures. Ask your fleet maintenance shop to run a <a class="text-primary hover:underline font-medium" style="color: #DC2626;" href="/semi-truck-diagnostics-in-phoenix-az">full computer diagnostic pull</a> at every major PM interval. Catching a pending EGR fault or a marginal NOx sensor before it triggers a limp mode on I-17 at 6 AM is the difference between a scheduled repair and a tow bill.</p>
<h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-foreground mt-6 mb-2">Build trailer maintenance into the contract explicitly</h3>
<p class="text-base leading-relaxed">Trailer issues are responsible for a significant portion of roadside OOS violations, but many small fleet operators only negotiate tractor service into their contracts. Make sure your agreement covers trailer brake inspection, ABS fault checks, lighting, and air-ride service. The <a href="https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/sites/fmcsa.dot.gov/files/docs/2017-CVSA-OOS-Criteria_Final-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener" class="underline">CVSA Out-of-Service Criteria</a> lists trailer brake and lighting defects among the most common OOS triggers — both of which are preventable with scheduled service.</p>
<h3 class="text-lg font-semibold text-foreground mt-6 mb-2">Coordinate drop-off windows around your dispatch schedule, not ours</h3>
<p class="text-base leading-relaxed">A good fleet maintenance program bends to your operation, not the other way around. CT Shop's 8-bay facility off I-17 near the I-10 interchange offers secure overnight truck parking, which means you can drop a truck after your last run, have it serviced during shop hours (Mon-Fri 8 AM–5 PM), and pick it up before the next dispatch. For operators running freight into Phoenix from Buckeye or Avondale, this overnight option keeps trucks on the road during revenue hours and in the shop during idle hours.</p>
